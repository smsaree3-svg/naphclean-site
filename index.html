How it works

It protects by slowly disappearing.

Naphthalene turns straight from solid to vapour. In a closed wardrobe that vapour keeps moths, silverfish and musty smells away. As it works the balls get smaller, which tells you when to top up.

  1. 01Pouch them4 to 6 balls in a small mesh or cloth pouch.
  2. 02Hang highHang the pouch near the top of the wardrobe. Never on the clothes.
  3. 03Keep it closedA closed door holds the vapour where it is needed.
  4. 04Top up on timeEvery 6 to 8 weeks, or when the balls look small.

How long does a pack last?
One placement protects a closed space for 6 to 8 weeks. A sealed pack keeps for 36 months from packing, so bigger packs are fine to stock up on.
Will it stain my clothes?
Classic White is non-staining. Multicolour balls can transfer colour if they touch fabric, so keep them in a pouch or a corner.
Is it safe? Can I use it in the kitchen?
Keep naphthalene away from children, pets and food. It is for wardrobes, cupboards, storage and washrooms only, never kitchens. Do not ingest. If swallowed, seek medical advice.
